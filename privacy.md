# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

PerfumeLab respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

When you use PerfumeLab, we may collect:

- Your email address (used for account authentication)
- User-generated content such as perfume formulas and ingredient data
- Basic technical information required for app functionality

We do not collect tracking data or use third-party advertising services.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

- Authenticate your account
- Store and sync your data securely
- Provide core app functionality

## Data Storage

Data is stored securely using Firebase services. Access to your data is restricted to your account.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app. This will permanently remove your account and associated data.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
